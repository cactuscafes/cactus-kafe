Account deletion is now offered directly inside the app. Completing it requires no
phone call, no email, no customer service contact and no website visit.

WHERE TO FIND IT
1. Open the "Sadakat Karti" (Loyalty Card) tab.
2. Create a card by entering a Turkish mobile number together with a name.
   No password and no sign-in is required.
   Test number: 0555 111 22 33
   (Our loyalty database stores Turkish mobile numbers, so the number must be in the
   05XX XXX XX XX format. The field accepts it typed as 05551112233, 5551112233 or
   +90 555 111 22 33 and normalises it automatically.)
3. On the card screen, scroll to the bottom.
4. Tap the red "Hesabimi Sil" ("Delete My Account") button.
5. A confirmation alert appears. Tap "Hesabimi Sil" to confirm.

WHAT DELETION DOES
The customer record, the star balance and the entire transaction history are
permanently removed from our server, and the locally cached card is cleared from the
device. The action is irreversible and completes entirely within the app.

"Cikis Yap" ("Log Out"), shown directly above the delete button, is a separate and
clearly labelled action that only signs out on this device. It is not offered as a
substitute for account deletion.
