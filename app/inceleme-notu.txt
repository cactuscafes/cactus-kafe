HOW TO TEST THE LOYALTY CARD
1. Open the "Sadakat Karti" (Loyalty Card) tab.
2. Enter a Turkish mobile number together with a name.
   Please use this test number: 0555 111 22 33
   (Our loyalty database stores Turkish mobile numbers, so the number must be
   in the 05XX XXX XX XX format. The app accepts it typed as 05551112233,
   5551112233 or +90 555 111 22 33 and normalises it for you.)
   No password or sign-in is required.

ACCOUNT DELETION (unchanged since 1.0, Guideline 5.1.1(v))
Account deletion is available directly inside the app. No phone call, no email
and no website visit is required.
1. Open the "Sadakat Karti" tab and create a card as described above.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the card screen.
3. Tap "Hesabimi Sil" ("Delete My Account") — the red button below "Cikis Yap".
4. Confirm in the alert that appears.

Deletion permanently removes the customer record, the star balance and the full
transaction history from our server, and clears the locally cached card. The
action is irreversible. "Cikis Yap" ("Log Out") above it is a separate, clearly
labelled action that only signs out on this device.

If the server cannot confirm the deletion, the app shows an error and keeps the
card instead of reporting a deletion that did not happen.

NATIVE FUNCTIONALITY
1. Loyalty Card tab — a fully native SwiftUI experience: create and view the
   card, see star progress and free-drink credits, show a scannable QR code and
   phone number at the counter, review transaction history, pull to refresh.
   Card data is cached on device, so the card remains available offline.
2. Contact tab — native actions: phone call, WhatsApp, Apple Maps directions,
   Instagram, and the system share sheet.
3. Home and Menu tabs — our website and live cafe menu.
