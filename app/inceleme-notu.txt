ACCOUNT DELETION (new in this build)
The Loyalty Card now offers account deletion directly inside the app. No phone call,
no email, and no website visit is required to complete it.

How to reach it:
1. Open the "Sadakat Karti" (Loyalty Card) tab.
2. Create a card by entering any phone number (at least 10 digits, for example
   5551112233) together with a name. No password or sign-in is required.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the card screen.
4. Tap "Hesabimi Sil" ("Delete My Account") — the red button below "Cikis Yap".
5. A confirmation alert appears; tap "Hesabimi Sil" to confirm.

What deletion does: the customer record, the star balance and the full transaction
history are permanently removed from our server, and the locally cached card is
cleared. The action is irreversible. "Cikis Yap" ("Log Out") above it is a separate,
clearly labelled action that only signs out on this device — it is not offered as a
substitute for deletion.

If the server cannot confirm the deletion, the app shows an error and keeps the card
instead of reporting a deletion that did not happen.

OTHER NATIVE FUNCTIONALITY (from the previous review, Guideline 4.2)
1. Loyalty Card tab — a fully native SwiftUI experience. Customers create and view
   their loyalty card inside the app, see their star progress and free-drink credits,
   display a scannable QR code and phone number at the counter, review their
   transaction history, and pull to refresh. Card data is cached on device, so the
   card remains available offline.
2. Contact tab — native actions: phone call, WhatsApp, Apple Maps directions,
   Instagram, and the system share sheet.
3. Menu tab — our live cafe menu.
