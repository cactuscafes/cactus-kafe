Hoop Coach — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Hoop Coach is a single-player basketball tactics game. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

What we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no registration. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location. We do not operate a server for this app, so no gameplay data ever leaves your device to us.

What stays on your device

  • Your chosen language, difficulty and match length
  • Nothing else — matches are not saved between sessions

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and advertising interaction data. This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

On first use after your first match, iOS asks whether you allow tracking. If you decline, the game works exactly the same — ads are simply not personalised. You can change this later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated.
