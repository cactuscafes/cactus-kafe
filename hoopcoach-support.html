Hoop Coach — Support

Turn-based basketball tactics. You don't play, you decide.

How the game works

Every possession you call a play. The opponent coach picks a defense at the same moment, without seeing your choice. The matchup between the two decides how likely the shot is to fall.

No play beats everything. Each defense leaves exactly one offense open — and the other way round. The opponent tracks what you call and starts countering your habits, so repeating a play stops working.

The opponent keeps guessing right. Am I doing something wrong?

You are probably repeating yourself. The AI weights recent calls most heavily. Change your pattern, or bait it: run the same play twice, then switch to the one that beats the coverage it just adopted.

My players get worse late in the game.

That's fatigue. Every possession tires the five on court and a tired player's ratings drop. Open Roster and substitute, or call a timeout to rest the whole team.

Matches feel too long or too short.

Main menu › Match Length. Quarters can be 3, 5, 8 or 12 minutes. Twelve is real quarter length and produces real basketball scores, but takes about twenty minutes.

Can I turn off the ads?

There is currently no paid option. Ads appear in the main menu and between quarters, at most twice per match. There are none during play.

How do I change the language?

Main menu › Language. English, Türkçe, Español and Deutsch. The change is immediate; no restart needed.
